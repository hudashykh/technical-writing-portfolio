# How Much Does a Website Design Cost in 2025?

*Blog post · SEO-focused explainer · Audience: small business owners planning a website*

> **Portfolio note:** Edited excerpt of the full post (introduction, cost breakdown and cost-saving tips). The cost figures are the ones used in the published article.

---

More than a billion websites exist today, and businesses keep updating or rebuilding theirs to keep up. Every rebuild means decisions about design trends, tools and features, all while staying on budget.

What you pay depends on four things: how complex the design is, how big the site is, which features you need, and how experienced the design team is. Website builders are very economical for basic sites. Custom development, advanced functionality and a polished design cost considerably more.

This guide walks through what drives the cost of a website in 2025, so you can plan your digital transformation with a realistic budget.

## How much does it cost to develop a website?

The cost ranges from a few dollars a month to well over $10,000. If you build a basic site yourself with a website builder, you can start from about $16 a month. If you want a custom design, professional help from designers, copywriters and photographers, and added functionality, the total can pass $10,000 quickly.

Here is what drives the price:

| Cost factor | Typical range | What to know |
|---|---|---|
| Website builder (Wix, Squarespace, WordPress) | $10–$150 per month | Cheapest route for a basic site |
| Website design and interface | $500–$10,000 | Depends on complexity and customization |
| Web hosting | $3–$500 per month | Shared hosting is cheapest; dedicated or cloud hosting suits high-traffic sites |
| Domain name | $10–$50 per year | Premium domains can cost thousands |
| Templates | $30–$200 | For a customized pre-made template |
| Content management system (CMS) | Free to premium | Premium themes, plugins and customization cost extra |
| SSL certificate | $10–$150 | Varies by provider and security features |
| Ecommerce functionality | $500–$30,000 | Depends on store size and features |
| Apps and integrations | $10–$500 per month | Email marketing, CRM and similar tools |
| Marketing | Varies widely | SEO retainers can run from hundreds to thousands of dollars a month; PPC and ads from a few dollars to thousands |

## How much does a website builder cost?

Website builders offer good value because they are simple to use. Plans run from about $10 a month for the basics to about $150 a month for multiple users, extra storage and more features. Ready-made templates let you build a professional-looking site without writing code. Match the plan to the features you will actually use.

## How much does a professional web designer cost?

If you have no time to build a site yourself, or you want a more creative look, hire a professional designer or developer. Three things set the price:

- **Complexity.** A small site with few pages costs less than one with advanced features, custom designs or third-party integrations.
- **Customization.** A unique, tailored design takes more time and effort, so it costs more.
- **Experience.** Senior developers charge more because they bring advanced skills and a record of high-quality work.

Free hosting options are available for all four types.

## How to reduce your website cost

You can keep costs down without sacrificing quality:

- **Choose shared hosting** if your traffic is modest, as long as performance holds up.
- **Use free templates and plugins** instead of paying for custom design or features you don't need yet.
- **Stick to built-in platform features** to minimize custom development.
- **Prioritize essential features** so add-ons don't quietly inflate your budget.
- **Review your subscriptions** and cancel unused or low-value tools.
- **Plan your content and layout first.** Changing a design or feature after development starts is expensive.
- **Optimize images and content** to improve speed and lower bandwidth costs.
- **Choose a cost-effective CMS** that you can manage within budget.
- **Use caching** to speed up the site, reduce server load and save on hosting.
- **Pay annually.** Most providers discount yearly plans compared with monthly billing.

## Final thoughts

A website in 2025 strengthens both your business and your brand. The final cost depends on complexity, design, functionality and ongoing maintenance, and it should follow what your audience actually needs. Use the ranges above to set a budget, then spend where it helps your customers most.
