## How I work

1. **Brief and audience first.** Who is reading, what do they need to decide, and what should they do next?
2. **Research.** I check facts, figures and terminology with subject experts and reliable sources before drafting.
3. **Structure for scanning.** Clear headings, short paragraphs, tables and lists where they help.
4. **SEO built in.** Target keywords go into headings and opening lines without hurting readability.
5. **Edit before delivery.** Every piece gets a read-through for accuracy, tone and grammar.
